Import Quantities from XML

Select an XML file containing product codes and quantities. Products are matched by their Code field.

Supported formats: <products>
  <product>
    <code>SKU-001</code>
    <quantity>100</quantity>
    <cost_per_unit>9.99</cost_per_unit>
  </product>
</products> or attribute-based: <product code="SKU-001" quantity="100" cost_per_unit="9.99"/>

Also accepts <cProd> in place of <code>, <qCom> in place of <quantity>, and <vUnCom> in place of <cost_per_unit>. The cost_per_unit field is optional.
